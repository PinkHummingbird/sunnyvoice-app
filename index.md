# SunnyVoice: Journal your Mood

**Talk for a minute. Understand yourself a little better.** Thought through on your iPhone, kept in your iCloud.

SunnyVoice is a voice journal for iPhone. Press record and talk; SunnyVoice writes down what you said and offers a gentle reflection and one small step — all on your iPhone. No account, no server of ours, no tracking. Your journal syncs only to your own private iCloud.
