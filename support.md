Questions, bug reports or feedback: support email coming soon

SunnyVoice is a voice journal for iPhone. Talk for a minute; it writes down what you said and reflects on it, all on your iPhone. There is no account, no server of ours and no data collection. Your journal syncs only to your own private iCloud.

**Not a crisis service.** If you're in danger, contact your local emergency number.

**Common questions**

- **Entries aren't showing on my other device.** Both devices need the same Apple ID, with iCloud on and Settings › iCloud › "Sync with iCloud" on in SunnyVoice. New entries usually arrive within a minute, but iCloud doesn't guarantee a time.
- **iCloud storage is full.** SunnyVoice keeps working and syncs once there is space. Recordings take about 240 KB per minute; you can turn off "Sync recordings" in Settings › iCloud.
- **Restoring Pro on a new iPhone.** Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.
- **Canceling a subscription.** Settings › Subscription › Manage subscription, or your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
- **Deleting everything.** Settings › iCloud › "Delete all iCloud data…" removes SunnyVoice's data from your iCloud; deleting the app removes it from the iPhone.
