## 2. Not therapy, not medical advice, not a crisis service

SunnyVoice offers reflections and small suggestions to help you think things through. They're created automatically on your iPhone and haven't been written or reviewed by a mental-health professional, so treat them as ideas to consider, not advice. SunnyVoice isn't therapy, a diagnosis or a crisis service, and it is not a medical device.

The safety check looks for phrases, so it can miss things and it can be wrong. Never rely on SunnyVoice in an emergency. **If you're in danger, contact your local emergency number.** If you're struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional.

Transcripts, moods, scores and reflections are produced automatically and can be inaccurate. Decisions you make about your health, relationships or anything else are yours.
